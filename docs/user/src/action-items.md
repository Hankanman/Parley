# Action items

Parley picks out action items, with owners and due dates when they're mentioned, whenever it summarises a meeting.

![The Action items page](/images/actions.png)

## In a meeting

Under the summary, the **Action Items** card lists the meeting's items. You can:

- tick an item off when it's done,
- **Edit** its text or **Delete** it,
- **Add item** by hand,
- **Extract from summary** (or **Re-extract**) to pick them out again.

Regenerating a summary replaces the items Parley found before, but keeps any you ticked off and never touches items you added yourself.

## All action items

**Action items** in the sidebar collects items from every meeting, grouped by meeting with the newest first. Filter by **Open**, **Done** or **All**, and click a meeting's name to open it.

## While recording (beta)

With **Settings → Recording → Live action items** turned on, Parley shows likely action items on the Record page during the meeting, updated every minute or so. These are a preview: the final list is created when the meeting is summarised.
